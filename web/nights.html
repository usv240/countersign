What the two clocks do and do not show

  • The left side is the public record. Where sources disagree on a time, the night says so below its timeline.
  • The right side starts when the record reaches Countersign. In real life it would arrive from the weather service's feed, or, for Hawaii, from the drill's script.
  • The paging uses short timeouts on this demo so a visitor can watch it move. The Kerr County slider shows the same workflow at a desk's real timeout. That part is arithmetic on the workflow's configuration, not a measurement, and it is labelled that way.
  • Countersign would not have stopped the rain. On Kerr County, it would have had a draft in front of a person within a minute of the first warning, and it would not have stopped paging until someone decided.
